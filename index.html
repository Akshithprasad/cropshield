// The Problem

The real reason crops fail

🛣️
54%
Rural roads unpaved

Produce rots before it reaches mandis. Refrigerated trucks can't operate on broken roads, turning good harvests into losses.

🏭
1:1.2L
Cold storage per farmers

Only one cold storage unit per 1.2 lakh farmers. Without cold chains, perishables spoil within 24–48 hours of harvest.

📡
25,000+
Villages with no internet

Farmers can't access price alerts, weather warnings, or government scheme updates. Invisible to the digital economy.

"This isn't a farming problem. It's an infrastructure problem. And infrastructure can be measured, mapped, and fixed."
// The Solution

How CropShield works

Step 01

Data Collection

Pulls road density, telecom coverage, cold storage capacity, and weather anomaly data from data.gov.in, TRAI, NHB, and IMD.

→
Step 02

Normalization

Each data point is cleaned and normalized to a 0–1 scale per district, making all factors comparable regardless of unit.

→
Step 03

Risk Scoring

A weighted formula combines all four factors into a single Infrastructure Crop Risk Index (0–100) per district.

→
Step 04

Visualization

Results shown on an interactive India map and district dashboard. Downloadable as CSV for policymakers and NGOs.

What this makes possible

For policymakers

Identify the top 20 highest-risk districts nationwide and direct infrastructure budgets where post-harvest loss is most likely to occur.

Deploy cold storage units, logistics support, and connectivity drives to villages flagged by CropShield before the season begins.

// The Purpose

Built for SDG 9

SDG 9 calls for resilient infrastructure, inclusive industrialization, and innovation. CropShield treats post-harvest loss as an infrastructure failure — not a farming one.


By surfacing data-driven gaps in roads, cold chains, and connectivity, it gives decision-makers the visibility to build the infrastructure India's farmers actually need. A blueprint that can scale across the Global South.

9.1 Develop resilient rural infrastructure to support economic development and human well-being
9.4 Upgrade infrastructure for sustainability, with greater resource efficiency
9.c Significantly increase access to ICT and universal internet access in least-developed regions
// About

Built by a student.
Driven by data.

CropShield was built as part of an application to the Future Minds Summit 2026, Thailand — a global forum for youth innovators working on the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The tool is open-source, data-driven, and built to be used by anyone who wants to fix India's post-harvest infrastructure gap.
